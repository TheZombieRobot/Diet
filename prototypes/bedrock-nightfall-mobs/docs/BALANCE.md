# Nightfall Mobs - the maths

> This file is generated by `npm run build` from `src/scripts/balance.js`. Change numbers there, not here.

## The target

"A little bit harder than the standard mobs, but not super hard." On **Normal** every new mob sits between a
zombie (20 health, 3 per hit) and an Enderman (40 health, 7 per hit), and each one has a clear weakness.
A player has 20 health (10 hearts). All numbers are health points: 2 points = 1 heart.

The posters gave damage as a range (for example Gloomhound 4-6). Each range became the difficulty ladder:
the poster's health is the Normal health, and the top of its damage range is the Extreme hit.

Why a crowd of 20-30 is survivable: Minecraft only lets a player be hurt once every half second, however many
mobs are swinging. So the worst the whole horde can do is two of its hardest hits per second - the
"worst case damage per second" column. A careless player standing still in the middle loses all 20 health in about
3 seconds on Normal with no armour (about 8 seconds in iron armour), which is frightening but leaves time to back away.
A door, a two-block wall or a pillar stops the horde completely: it cannot open or break doors and cannot break blocks.

- The horde arrives in 3 groups, 3, 6, 9 seconds after the call, 20-32 blocks from the hunted player and never within 16 blocks of any player.
- Grasper slow: Slowness I for 1s (Easy), 1s (Normal), 1.5s (Hard), 2s (Extreme), at most once every 5 seconds per player.
- One Stalker calls one horde, once. No call happens if more than 8 horde mobs are already within 64 blocks, and there are never more than 45 alive.
- Horde mobs and the Stalker burn in daylight, like zombies, so a horde never outlasts the night.
- Loot is shared by the whole horde: a few random members carry one item each. The "most loot items" column is the absolute cap.
  A vanilla zombie gives 5 XP, so 22 zombies would give 110; the Normal horde gives 59 - worth fighting, not worth farming.

## Drops and crafting

- Gloomhound: string, Gloom Fang 50%, Shadow Hide 20%. A Shadow Hood needs 2 hides, 2 fangs and 1 string: about 10 hounds.
- Thornskulk: sticks, Thorn Core 40%, Bark Hide 20%. A Bramble Charm needs 2 hides, 1 core and 1 string: about 10 skulks.
- Horde Stalker: rotten flesh, Horde Claw 50%, Tattered Cloth 20%.
- Shadow Hood: helmet with 2 armour (like chainmail) and Night Vision while worn.
- Bramble Charm: held in the off hand, anything that hits you takes 2 damage.

## How often they appear

Spawn weights next to vanilla (zombie 100, Enderman 10). All three only appear on the surface in the dark.

- Gloomhound: weight 10, groups of 1-2, at most 2 nearby, biomes tagged plains or forest.
- Thornskulk: weight 8, groups of 1-1, at most 2 nearby, biomes tagged forest or taiga.
- Horde Stalker: weight 6, groups of 1-1, at most 1 nearby, biomes tagged monster.

These numbers have been checked by the automated tests but **not yet play-tested in the real game**. Expect to tune them after the first family session.
