# Data Model (plain language)

All data lives in the phone's IndexedDB in one database named `diet`, with versioned migrations from day one. Schema version 1 was built at checklist item 12; every store's fields, ranges and indexes are defined once in `packages/core/src/storage` (`records.ts` and `specs/v1.ts`), and the database, repositories and backup screens are in `apps/mobile-web/src/db`. Each table below is a list of records (a "store"). Fields marked *derived* are calculated, not stored. Every field is always present: a field that can be empty holds null, never "missing". Every value is plain JSON (text, finite numbers, true/false, null, lists and objects of these), so a backup gives back exactly what was stored. Times are stored in UTC with the device's time zone recorded, so a fast that crosses midnight or daylight saving is measured correctly.

There are 28 stores. 26 go into a backup; `transfer_pairings` and `garden_photos` never leave the phone. The seed datasets (about 150 ingredients, the recipes, the exercises and session templates in `packages/core/data/exercises.json`, and the drink-picker serves) live in the app's code with `seed:` ids, not in the database; the owner's corrections to a seed food are small patches in `ingredient_overrides`.

No record, or a record under an older wording, means unanswered, which counts as yes. Re-answering question 3 or 6 deletes its confirmation, so it is asked again. Question 7 reads "Do you drink alcohol most days, or heavily at weekends?" |
| **targets** | Current numeric targets | protein g range, your keto net-carb ceiling (20–50 g; Kickstart's 20 g is derived), moderate low-carb ceiling (50–130 g), saturated-fat budget %, Kickstart deficit % (up to 30) and Steady deficit % (15–20), energy floor kJ (5,000 or higher; it can only be raised), fibre guide g and water target mL (empty means the default for your sex), eating window preset and window clock times (empty until chosen, or while fasting is switched off at onboarding; an empty preset with a confirmed schedule means no fasting plan), how the preset was chosen, reflux options, updated. *Derived:* phase, phase start and extensions (from the two Kickstart tables) |

**Shared rules enforced in `packages/core`:**
- Standard drinks: `ml * abv * 0.789 / 1000`, rounded to 2 decimals; label value wins if present.
- Eating window length is computed from clock times, wrapping midnight correctly; fasting hours = 24 − window. Presets and their default clock times: 16:8 11:00–19:00, 18:6 13:00–19:00, 20:4 15:00–19:00, 22:2 17:00–19:00, 23:1 18:00–19:00.
- Single-meal check (one-meal windows under 2 hours, or a day with one planned meal): the 5 pm snack is offered when the planned sitting's protein is below the protein target (the range maximum in Kickstart, the minimum in Steady), or the day's planned food energy is below the day's energy target (energy with no target available counts as not met), or the sitting needs more than about 600 g of cooked protein food; and, through the day, when what is logged so far plus the meals still planned falls below either target (alcohol is never counted). Strict, no tolerance. The 5,000 kJ floor only refuses or warns, and the eating window never changes the targets.
- Fasting span, planned fast and streak: fasts less than an hour apart form one span for the elapsed time, the over-24 h warning, the plan cap and the streak. A fast is planned from its real start to the next eating window, capped at 24 hours from the span start. A completed span of 24 hours or less credits the day it ends; the days of a span over 24 hours, or still running, are neutral (they neither add to the streak nor end it).
- Net carbs = carbs − fibre, floored at zero.
- Energy estimate = Mifflin-St Jeor × activity factor (1.2 sedentary, 1.55 moderate, 1.725 physically active job, 1.9 very active), labelled "rough guide".
- Planned energy = ⌈estimate × (100 − deficit %) ÷ 100⌉ kJ, never below the floor (5,000 kJ or the owner's higher floor); with no estimate there is no target, no planner aim and no meal-check figure, and the floor still refuses and warns; protein target is independent of the deficit (item 21).
- The estimate's weight is the 7-day average, or the highest weigh-in of the last 7 days, or the last weigh-in. The Kickstart deficit is 30% by default (0–30%), the Steady deficit 15% (15–20%); there is no deficit while readiness answer 1, 2 or 5 is yes or unanswered, nor for the rest of a Kickstart after a stop-sign check.
- Effective protein (C65): meals are checked against the top of the range while Kickstart figures apply, and against the bottom of it otherwise.
- Net-carb ceilings: keto 30 g by default (20–50 g; 20 g during Kickstart; held at 50 g for readiness answer 3 until the prescriber confirmation), moderate low-carb 100 g (50–130 g), none in balanced; every ceiling is off for readiness answers 1, 2 and 5, and suggestions are ordered by the ceiling in effect, never filtered.
- Saturated-fat budget = planned energy × % ÷ 37 kJ per g, rounded down. Fibre guide 30 g (men) or 25 g (women); water guide 2,600 mL or 2,100 mL; either can be the owner's own figure.
- Alcohol energy is shown as its own part of the day's total and never counts toward the floor, the planner or the meal checks (HS-36).
- Phase: Kickstart for 21 days from the day the owner starts it (plus any one-week extensions confirmed after the four stop-sign questions; at most 3 extensions and 42 days in all, across every Kickstart), then Steady. Kickstart runs only while keto mode and the readiness gates allow it (and keto has not been left and re-entered since it began); otherwise the end is written with its reason (readiness-gated, then left-keto) and Kickstart never restarts by itself. A natural end is never written: the phase is read from the stored start and extensions. A Kickstart whose first day has not come by the phone's date (the clock was ahead when it was started) is not running: the phase is Steady until that day, no start is offered, and it can be ended (and is, when its conditions fail).
- Projection band (item 14): a range, never a single target line. Steady edges run at 0.5 kg a week and at the lower of 1.0 kg a week and the rapid-loss threshold in use. In the first Kickstart ever, and only when it started the carb ceiling (first low-carb day within 24 hours of its start), a one-off water and glycogen offset of 1.0 to 1.5 kg is spread evenly over days 0 to 14, so the band is front-loaded and then flattens. The four-week chart is aligned to the start of the current phase and re-anchored every 28 days at the 7-day average (else the latest weigh-in of the week before, else the first weigh-in in the block's first 3 days). The band is hidden when readiness closes the deficit, when no carb ceiling is active, and when weight is below BMI 18.5. The chart's vertical span is never under 2 kg.
- Long-term goal and next milestone: the long-term goal must be below the current weight and at or above BMI 18.5 for the height (goal rounded down and height up to 0.1). The next milestone is worked out, never entered: the goal, or 80% of the higher of the 7-day average and the latest weigh-in of the last 14 days rounded up to 0.1 kg, whichever is higher.
- Rapid-loss card: shown when the 7-day average fell faster than the threshold (1 kg a week by default, strictly greater; adjustable only down to 0.75 kg and to 2 weeks) in each of the last 3 rolling weeks ending today; a week containing one of the first 21 Kickstart days ever does not count, and a week's previous window is not clipped. Dismissal hides it until local midnight only.
- Fasting preset: an untouched 23:1 Kickstart default is used as 22:2 (17:00–19:00) whenever no Kickstart is running, and is then stored as 22:2 with an explanation; a preset the owner chose is never moved. A Kickstart start moves an untouched default to 23:1, except when readiness answer 4 is yes or unanswered; while it is, a stored 23:1 Kickstart default is used as 22:2 even during Kickstart, and any pattern the owner saves is recorded as their own.
- Portion scaling: user portion = recipe per-serve × my portion fraction; household servings never change the user's numbers.
- Confidence: label > nz-fcdb or usda > estimate; a recipe's confidence is the weakest of its ingredients.
- Grocery aggregation: same ingredient merged across recipes, converted to grams first, then to pack counts rounded up; garden and pantry subtracted before pack rounding; each deduction writes an audit row.
- Times: every event record (a weigh-in, fast, drink, food entry, symptom, Kickstart, check, audit line or card dismissal) stores a UTC instant and the device's IANA time zone at that moment; a fast and a Kickstart also store the zone they ended in. Day-only records (planned days, steps, sessions, alcohol day notes) are keyed by the local date. Bookkeeping times (created, updated, answered, last backup) store no zone. "Fewer than 14 days" and "after 56 days" style rules count elapsed 24-hour periods, so travel cannot shift them.
- Zone fallback: a record is read in its own recorded zone. A zone this browser does not know (recorded under newer time-zone data, then restored elsewhere) falls back to the home zone, then Pacific/Auckland, then UTC, and the screen says "Times shown in your home time zone". New records only ever take a zone the device knows.
- Drinking day: drinks before the cut-over (default 5 am) count toward the previous evening, worked out on the wall clock, never by subtracting hours, so daylight-saving nights come out right. A date skipped by crossing the date line does not break a run of drinking days; a day you lived through with nothing logged does.
- "Collapsed for the day" card dismissals last until the next local midnight (the alcohol help card: until the next drinking-day cut-over) in the zone where the card was dismissed.
- A beverage preset counts as "verified" (NN-01) when its kcal comes from the label.

**Export formats**
- Backup: one JSON file of every backed-up table, named `diet-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` (`-locked` when locked; a rescue copy adds `-rescue`), dated with the local date of the export. `.gitignore` and the repository scan keep these names out of git. The file is an envelope:

  | Field | Plain file | Locked file |
  |---|---|---|
  | format | the app's backup marker (diet-backup) | the same |
  | version | 1 (the envelope version) | 1 |
  | encrypted | false | true |
  | payload | schema version, kind (backup or rescue), exported at (UTC), app version, and tables: every backed-up table, in a fixed order, an empty list when it has no records | absent: it is inside the ciphertext |
  | kdf | absent | PBKDF2, SHA-256, iterations (600,000 when made; any figure from 600,000 to 10,000,000 is accepted), a 16-byte salt |
  | cipher | absent | AES-GCM, 256-bit key, a 12-byte IV, 128-bit tag |
  | ciphertext | absent | the encrypted payload; the header values above are bound to it, so changing any of them makes the file unopenable |

  Each record is built only from its table's own field list (the allow-list), so nothing else can leak into a file. Two things are deliberately left out, as whole tables: **the laptop pairing (`transfer_pairings`) and garden photos (`garden_photos`)**; a restore always leaves the app unpaired, and keeps a photo only when its garden record comes back (a same-phone restore). Every other record round-trips exactly (HS-15). The password (10 to 1,000 characters, trimmed) is never stored; a forgotten one cannot be recovered. Before offering a file, the app checks it with its own import rules, and decrypts a locked one again to compare. A backup counts (settings, last backup made) only once you say you can see the file.
- Restore: refuses a file over 50 MB, anything that is not this app's backup, a newer or unknown version, a damaged or wrongly unlocked file, any record outside its allowed fields or ranges (a safety value outside its bounds refuses the whole file; nothing is clamped), and records that do not fit together. An older file is migrated first, and any value changed to fit new limits is listed and kept as an audit line. A restore replaces everything in one step: if anything fails, nothing changes and the records shown on the confirmation can be saved as a rescue copy.
- Rescue copy: offered when an update cannot convert the records (read with plain IndexedDB at the installed version), when the export's own check fails ("Save an unchecked copy"), and after a failed restore. It uses the same file format with kind "rescue", locked by default, built from that version's field lists without other checks. Restoring one keeps every record that passes and lists the others by table and position. It never counts as a backup.
- Grocery CSV: `aisle,item,quantity,unit,pack_suggestion,from_garden,substitute,price,price_entered_at,ticked`.
- Grocery JSON: the list record as stored, with the store name and timestamps.
- Transfer payload (phone to laptop): the grocery list JSON, compressed, encrypted; includes a list id and version so re-sending never duplicates on the laptop.

**Extension storage (laptop):** the last received list with tick state and notes, the pairing record, and the mode (A/B/C). Nothing else.
